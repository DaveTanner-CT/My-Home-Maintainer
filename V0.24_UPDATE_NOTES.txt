HomeMaintainer v0.24 - Room Inches Normalization

ROOM DIMENSIONS
- Corrected feet/inches entry behavior for Length, Width, and Ceiling Height.
- The inches field now accepts total inches, including values greater than 11.
- Examples:
  38 inches -> 3 ft 2 in
  74 inches -> 6 ft 2 in
  120 inches -> 10 ft 0 in
- If a feet value is also entered, total inches are added to it correctly.
  Example: 5 ft + 26 in -> 7 ft 2 in.
- The app preserves the raw number while the user is typing, then normalizes the display when the inches field loses focus.
- Meter entry remains decimal and is unchanged.

BASE BUILD
- Preserves all v0.23 sample-data cleanup behavior.
- Preserves v0.22 connected-flow improvements and all prior room features.

VALIDATION
- All Swift files were parsed successfully with swiftc -parse before packaging.
