HomeMaintainer v0.30 - Connected History & Actionable Room Summaries

- Adds prefilled Add History Event actions directly from Room, Home System, Device/Equipment, Fixture, and Project detail pages.
- Maintenance history matching now prefers typed SwiftData relationships for systems/devices while retaining legacy-name fallback.
- Project detail now includes its connected Home History records.
- Room At a Glance detector and consumable counts are now tappable and open the exact related records.
- Room warranty warnings are now tappable and open the exact systems/devices/fixtures needing attention.
- Preserves all v0.29.1 functionality and updates visible/internal version metadata to v0.30.
