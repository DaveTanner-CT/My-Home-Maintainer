HomeMaintainer v0.31.1 - Maintenance Action Workflow

- Consumable detail pages now include Mark Replaced Today.
- Marking a consumable replaced updates Last Replaced and calculates Next Replacement from the saved interval.
- The same action automatically creates a Home History record so replacements are not lost as silent date changes.
- Consumables can create a replacement reminder task directly from the item page when a next replacement date is known.
- Detector detail pages can create a replacement reminder directly from the recorded replacement date.
- Detector and consumable detail pages can create a prefilled Home History event without navigating away to the general history screen.
- MaintenanceRecordFormView now accepts a prefilled related-item name/task title for record types that do not have their own direct history relationship yet.
- Preserves all v0.30 relationship, multi-room, setup, photo, and history improvements.
- Visible/internal build metadata updated to v0.31.1.
