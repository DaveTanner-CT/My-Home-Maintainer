My Home Keeper v0.41
Account Foundation

- Adds Sign in with Apple to Settings > Account.
- Persists the Apple user identifier securely in the iOS Keychain.
- Stores the name/email Apple shares on first authorization for future sessions.
- Checks Apple credential state on app launch and when Account opens.
- Adds local sign out without touching existing home data.
- Keeps all current home records local; no cloud migration occurs in v0.41.
- Adds the Sign in with Apple entitlement plumbing for the iOS target.
- Preserves universal iPhone/iPad targeting, orientation metadata, AppIcon configuration, and TestFlight version metadata.
- Version: 0.41, build 410.

Apple Developer setup required before distribution signing:
1. Enable Sign in with Apple on the App ID org.scriptingforschools.HomeMaintainer.
2. Regenerate/download the App Store provisioning profile in Codemagic after enabling the capability.
