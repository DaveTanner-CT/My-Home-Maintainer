HomeMaintainer v0.20
Room Dimensions + Room Photo Grid

Base: HomeMaintainer v0.19

ROOM DETAIL CHANGES
- Preserved all existing Room Detail sections and + menu actions:
  Projects, Paint & Finishes, Home Systems, Devices & Equipment, Fixtures,
  Tasks, Recent Home History, and Documents.
- Added editable room dimensions directly inside the Area section:
  Dimension Units (Feet or Meters), Length, Width, Ceiling Height.
- Added calculated floor area (sq ft or m²).
- Switching Feet/Meters converts existing saved room measurements.
- Added Room Photos immediately below Area.
- Room photos render as thumbnail-only grids: 2 columns on compact layouts,
  3 columns on regular layouts.
- Tapping a thumbnail opens the existing Attachment Detail / zoomable photo flow.
- Photo captions/descriptions remain editable from photo details.
- Room photos no longer repeat in the bottom attachment list; the bottom section
  is now Documents for rooms.
- Add Room Photo remains directly available from the Room Photos section.

DATA / EXPORT
- Added optional Room dimension fields to SwiftData for lightweight migration.
- Home Transfer import/export now preserves room dimension unit and measurements.
- Home Export archives now include room dimension unit and measurements.
- Build number updated to 0.20 in Settings.

VALIDATION
- All Swift source files passed `swiftc -parse` syntax validation in the package.
