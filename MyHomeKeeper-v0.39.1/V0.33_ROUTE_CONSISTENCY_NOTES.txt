HomeMaintainer v0.33 - Route Consistency & Photo Capture

Purpose
- Ensure record creation/editing exposes the same core capabilities no matter where the form is launched.

Changes
- Added reusable pending-photo picker to Add/Edit forms for:
  * Fixtures
  * Home Systems
  * Devices & Equipment
  * Smoke & CO Detectors
  * Filters & Consumables
  * Tasks
  * Vendors
  * Home History Events
- Photos selected during creation are attached to the new record when Save is tapped.
- Existing records can add another photo from Edit without requiring a detour to the detail page.
- Room, Paint/Finish, Project, and Project Item retain their specialized existing photo workflows.
- Verified room-launched forms and list-launched forms use the same underlying form types; room launch only preselects room context.

Consistency rule
If a record supports Photos & Documents on its detail page, the Add/Edit form now offers photo capture/selection as part of the same workflow.
