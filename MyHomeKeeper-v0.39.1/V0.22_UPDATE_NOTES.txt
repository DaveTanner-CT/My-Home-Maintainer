HomeMaintainer v0.22 - Dimensions + Connected Summary Flows

Base: HomeMaintainer v0.21.

ROOM DIMENSIONS
- Feet mode now uses separate feet and inches fields for Length, Width, and Ceiling Height.
- Inches normalize automatically (for example, 14 inches becomes 1 ft 2 in).
- Meters remains decimal entry.
- Existing decimal-feet values remain compatible and are displayed as feet/inches.
- Switching between Feet and Meters continues to convert saved measurements.

CONNECTED FLOW AUDIT
- Home Outlook: missing dates, missing service-life estimates, and missing recorded costs are now tappable and show the exact records needing updates.
- Home Setup: connection/detail warnings now open filtered lists of the exact affected records rather than a broad category list.
- Home Insights: health counts now navigate to the relevant warranty, detector, consumable, project, attachment, or history records.
- Home Dashboard: Overdue, Current, and Upcoming summary cards now open the exact tasks represented by each count.
- Room At a Glance: project and task counts now open room-specific lists.

PRESERVED
- Room inline Add controls for projects, paint/finishes, systems, devices/equipment, fixtures, and tasks.
- Room photo thumbnail grid and zoom/detail behavior.
- Home Transfer/export support for room dimensions.

VALIDATION
- Swift source files parsed with swiftc -parse after changes.
