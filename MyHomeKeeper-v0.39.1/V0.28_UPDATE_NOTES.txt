HomeMaintainer v0.28 - Multi-Room Editing

WHAT CHANGED
- Added a reusable Rooms / Areas multi-selection control.
- Home Systems can now be assigned to multiple rooms directly from Add/Edit System.
- Applied the same multi-room editing experience to Devices & Equipment, Fixtures, Paint / Finish, Projects, and Tasks.
- The existing primary-room field remains internal for backward compatibility; users simply check every applicable room.
- Removing the current primary room automatically promotes another selected room when available.
- Existing multi-room relationships are loaded into the forms and preserved when editing.
- Task room selection can inherit all rooms from a linked System, Device/Equipment, Fixture, or Project when no room has yet been selected.
- Build/home/export/transfer version updated to 0.28.

VALIDATION
- All Swift source files pass swiftc -parse.
