My Home Keeper v0.42 — Household Foundation

- Adds local Household, HouseholdMember, and HouseholdInvitation SwiftData models.
- A signed-in user can adopt the existing local Home into a Household without moving or duplicating existing records.
- The adopting user becomes the Household Owner.
- Adds Owner / Editor / Viewer role groundwork.
- Owners can prepare family invitation records with role and invitation code.
- Adds Household screens to Settings and Account.
- Existing home data remains local and unchanged in v0.42.
- Cross-device synchronization and real invitation acceptance are intentionally deferred to v0.43 cloud sync.
- Version: 0.42, build 420.
