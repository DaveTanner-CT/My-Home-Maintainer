HomeMaintainer v0.26 - Paint Mixing Labels + Room Locations

PAINT / FINISHES
- Added PhotosPicker support directly in Add/Edit Paint.
- New Mixing Label section allows a photo of the paint mixing label to be added while the paint record is being created.
- Editing a paint can replace the existing Mixing Label photo rather than creating duplicates.
- Mixing label is saved as a normal HomeAttachment owned by the PaintFinish record, so the existing attachment viewer/zoom behavior remains available.

SMOKE & CO DETECTORS
- Added a SwiftData Room relationship to Detector.
- Add/Edit Detector now uses a Room / Area picker populated from the home's Room records.
- Existing detector records with legacy free-text locations attempt to resolve automatically to a matching Room by name.
- Detector list/detail views show the linked room.
- Room renames keep the detector legacy location text synchronized.

FILTERS & CONSUMABLES
- Added a SwiftData Room relationship to Consumable.
- Add/Edit Consumable now includes a Room / Area picker populated from the home's Room records.
- Consumable list/detail views show the linked room.

DATA INTEGRITY / PORTABILITY
- Room deletion unlinks Detectors and Consumables instead of deleting those records.
- Home Transfer preserves Detector and Consumable room relationships.
- Home Export includes room names for Detector and Consumable snapshots.
- App/build/export version updated to 0.26.

VALIDATION
- All Swift source files passed swiftc -parse.
