HomeMaintainer v0.34 - Furniture + Ownership Transfer Selection

NEW FURNITURE FOUNDATION CATEGORY
- Added Furniture immediately after Fixtures in Home Setup > Foundation.
- Furniture supports:
  - category
  - brand / maker
  - model / collection
  - serial / inventory ID
  - material / finish
  - dimensions
  - purchase date / price / store
  - warranty expiration
  - product/reference link
  - notes
  - multiple Rooms / Areas
  - vendor
  - related project
  - photos and documents
  - Home History entries
- Furniture can be created or linked from a Room page.
- Furniture appears in room asset summaries, room history context, warranty alerts, global search, Home Setup connection checks, project completion, standard export, and Home Transfer.

OWNER TRANSFER ITEM SELECTION
- Creating an Owner Transfer now opens an "Items Staying With Home" screen first.
- Furniture, Fixtures, and Devices & Equipment are listed item-by-item with checkboxes.
- All items are selected by default; the current owner can uncheck any item that will not stay with the home.
- Excluded movable records are omitted from the transfer package.
- Photos/documents owned by excluded movable records are also omitted.
- Tasks and Home History are retained for continuity, but their direct link to an excluded movable item is not exported.
- Older transfer files remain compatible because Furniture is an optional transfer-array field.

VERSION
- Home page, Settings, normal export, and transfer metadata updated to v0.34.
